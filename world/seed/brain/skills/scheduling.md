# How I schedule

- Default meeting length 30 minutes; investor and design reviews can be 60.
- No meetings before 9:30am or after 6pm, except family and friends.
- Friday afternoons (after 1pm) are deep work — decline unless it's Priya or Daniel.
- When moving a meeting, update the existing event rather than creating a duplicate, and keep
  the attendees on it.
- Launch week (Oct 5–9): only launch-related meetings.
