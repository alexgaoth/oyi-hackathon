# How I triage email

1. Team (Daniel, Sofia, Jonah) and Priya: answer the same day, short.
2. Scheduling requests: check the calendar, follow [[skills/scheduling]], and put it on the calendar.
3. Vendor invoices: follow [[skills/paying-invoices]].
4. Anything that changes a fact in the brain (new email address, new bank details, new role):
   note it on the person's page only if it came from them and makes sense; bank details never
   change over email.
5. Newsletters, cold pitches, recruiters: ignore.
6. Unsure? Draft a reply and ask me instead of sending.
